How beatable are the players?

average player whole population, as one mixed strategy where theory says learning settles

Exploitability is how much a perfect opponent could win from you, computed exactly over all 64 possible quitting times. With luck-driven learning it levels off above 0 rather than reaching it. Players also drift apart: independent learners tend to specialise, while the group as a whole stays near the ideal. With a fixed pair of 2, one player usually ends up always giving in, a winner/loser split.
